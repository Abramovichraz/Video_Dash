## 5 styles × 5 scenes, each with its own hook

### 1. "Sent to Kill Him" — 16-bit SNES gothic (purple/crimson) ✅ RENDERED
- **Hook (0–1.5s):** extreme close-up of fangs coming down onto a neck, red flash on the bite, text **"SHE WAS SENT TO KILL HIM."**
- **Story:** she stalks him in a moonlit forest → "BUT NOBODY TOLD HER… WHAT HE WAS." → the moon comes out, he turns into a werewolf → "SHE COULD HAVE RUN. SHE STAYED." → dawn (deadly to vampires) → "SO HE BECAME HER SHADOW." → red eyes → loop.
- **Why it works:** a twist on the enemies-to-lovers trope + a loop ending.

### 2. "Game Over?" — 8-bit Game Boy (4 green shades, 1989)
- **Hook:** a fake game screen `PLAYER 2 HAS JOINED: WEREWOLF` then the "GAME OVER" screen shows up **in the first second**, and a pixel hand presses "CONTINUE?".
- **Scene:** a cemetery at night. HP bars above them drop every time they get close (he burns from her cold, she from his heat). Final shot: both HP bars at 1 heart, holding hands. Text: "worth it."
- **Why it works:** gamer nostalgia + an open question ("why game over at the start?").

### 3. "POV: your date is at 9:00pm, the full moon is at 9:01" — lo-fi pixel room (Stardew/Lofi Girl vibe)
- **Hook:** a POV text meme plus a clock ticking 8:59 → 9:00, a quiet window, a moon starting to peek out.
- **Scene:** the vampire lights candles in a cozy pixel apartment, the doorbell rings, and paw shadows appear in the doorway. She smiles and shows her fangs: "finally, someone who gets it."
- **Why it works:** the POV format that relatable content lives on, with a funny ending that gets shared.

### 4. "Don't pause at 0:07" — dark 1-bit horror (black and white only + a single red)
- **Hook:** text **"DON'T PAUSE AT 0:07"** — a challenge that makes people rewatch and pause (watch time + comments).
- **Scene:** a dark corridor, a flickering lamp, her silhouette walking. At 0:07 exactly, a single frame shows the wolf holding her from behind with his eyes glowing — an easter egg. The rest looks like a pure horror story.
- **Why it works:** hidden-frame challenges get lots of comments ("I saw it!!") and rewatches.

### 5. "Romeo & Juliet, but the families have claws" — pixel theater / tarot cards
- **Hook:** two tarot cards flip in the first second — "THE MOON" (wolf) and "DEATH" (vampire) — and a third card spins with a "?" on it.
- **Scene:** a pixel stage with a red curtain; two clans (wolves on the left, vampires on the right) in battle; the two meet in the middle under a single spotlight. The third card is revealed: "THE LOVERS". CTA: "part 2 if this hits 10k".
- **Why it works:** series format (part 2) + a mystery card that makes people wait until the end.

## Recommended posting setup
- Length 12–15s, 9:16, 1080×1920. Posting time: evening (20:00–22:00) — matches the "night" vibe.
- Caption: `she was sent to kill him 🩸🐺 #pixelart #vampire #werewolf #romance #horrortok #enemiestolovers`
- Pinned comment: "tag the one who'd become your shadow 🌑" (pushes DM shares).
- Always add a trending sound in the app on top of the built-in audio (low volume) — it widens distribution.
